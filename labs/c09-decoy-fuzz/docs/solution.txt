Ports    : HTTP 18160, HTTPS 18523

Verify   : make verify LAB=c09-decoy-fuzz      (15 assertions)

OBJECTIVE
  Same sweep as C03 against an adversarial set: 39 look-alikes, including catch-alls
  that answer 200 to every path and 8 hosts whose headers claim to be the real one.
  The grade is precision.

STEP 1 - enumerate the candidates
  jq -r '.sites[].host' labs/c09-decoy-fuzz/edge/sites.json
  expected: 40 lines (chatadmin + 39 decoys)
  The unbounded label list is _shared/data/vhost-labels.txt plus app01..app16.

STEP 2 - score on body content, never on status
  for h in $(jq -r '.sites[].host' labs/c09-decoy-fuzz/edge/sites.json); do
    b=$(curl -sk -H "Host: $h" https://127.0.0.1:18523/app)
    case "$b" in *"Chat Admin"*) echo "HIT $h";; esac
  done
  expected: HIT chatadmin.corp.lab      (exactly one line)

STEP 3 - see the traps fail
  Catch-alls answer 200 everywhere:
    curl -sk -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code}\n' -H 'Host: monitor.corp.lab' \
      https://127.0.0.1:18523/app
    expected: 200
    curl -sk -H 'Host: monitor.corp.lab' https://127.0.0.1:18523/app | grep -c 'Chat Admin'
    expected: 0
    (same for status.corp.lab)

  Lures claim the real host in a response header:
    for l in vpn intranet owa citrix sso webmail remote files; do
      curl -sk -D - -o /dev/null -H "Host: $l.corp.lab" https://127.0.0.1:18523/ \
        | tr -d '\r' | sed -n 's/^[Xx]-[Ff]orwarded-[Hh]ost: //p'
    done | sort -u
    expected: chatadmin.corp.lab     (8 hosts say it, none of them is it)

STEP 4 - the roots are byte-identical
  for h in $(jq -r '.sites[].host' labs/c09-decoy-fuzz/edge/sites.json | grep -v chatadmin); do
    curl -sk -H "Host: $h" https://127.0.0.1:18523/ | shasum -a1
  done | sort -u | wc -l
  expected: 1

STEP 5 - the wall still holds for anything unlisted
  curl -skI -H 'Host: not-in-scope.corp.lab' https://127.0.0.1:18523/ | grep -Ei 'HTTP/|server'
  expected: 404 + server: Microsoft-HTTPAPI/2.0

STEP 6 - regenerate and confirm determinism (optional)
  python3 labs/c09-decoy-fuzz/gen.py
  expected: "generated: 1 real + 39 decoys (8 lures, 2 catch-alls)"
  Re-run verify: the tree is identical.

WHY IT MATTERS
  A vhost finding is a body/behaviour claim supported by two paths. Anything resting
  on one 200, one header, or one length difference is the kind of thing that gets
  closed as informational. The grade here is zero false positives, not a hit count.

TEARDOWN
